  <h2 id="research">Research directions <span class="subtle">(연구 분야)</span></h2>
  <p>RIRO Lab aims to make robots trusted collaborators across society through advances in learning, reasoning, and execution. We are particularly interested in collaborative mobile manipulation that combines learning, planning, and communication.</p>
  <p>Potential research directions include, but are not limited to:</p>
  <div class="card-grid">
    <article class="card">
      <h3>Robot Foundation Models &amp; Humanoids</h3>
      <p>Develop vision-language-action (VLA) models and robot foundation models for humanoid robots.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="card">
      <h3>Reinforcement &amp; Constraint Learning</h3>
      <p>Study reinforcement learning and inverse reinforcement learning for manipulation and constraint learning.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="card">
      <h3>Multi-Agent Systems &amp; Grounding</h3>
      <p>Explore multi-agent systems, simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM), or natural-language grounding.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="card">
      <h3>Algorithms on Real Robots</h3>
      <p>Design, implement, and evaluate algorithms on the lab's experimental robotic platforms.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
  <p style="margin-top: 18px;">Explore our <a href="/research/topics">research areas</a> and <a href="/publication/international">publications</a> to see how your work connects with the lab.</p>

  <h2 id="environment">Research environment <span class="subtle">(연구 환경)</span></h2>
  <p>You will collaborate with multiple graduate students, with a focus on algorithm design and implementation. The lab also maintains collaborations with research groups in Korea and internationally.</p>
  <p>Visit our <a href="/research/equipments/">Equipment</a> page for current information about the lab's robotic platforms and experimental facilities.</p>

  <h2 id="profile">Who should apply? <span class="subtle">(지원 자격)</span></h2>
  <h3>Required qualifications</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Doctoral degree:</strong> A Ph.D. in robotics, AI, or a closely related field.</li>
    <li><strong>Research record:</strong> A strong academic record supported by publications.</li>
    <li><strong>Technical foundation:</strong> A strong background in at least one of planning, reinforcement learning, computer vision, deep learning, control, or natural language processing.</li>
    <li><strong>Programming:</strong> Proficiency in Python, Linux, and deep learning or reinforcement learning frameworks such as PyTorch or TensorFlow.</li>
    <li><strong>Communication:</strong> Good written and spoken English.</li>
  </ul>
  <h3>Additional strengths</h3>
  <ul>
    <li>Familiarity with state-of-the-art robot learning methods.</li>
    <li>Experience with real robot systems, particularly manipulators or humanoids.</li>
    <li>A collaborative approach to research and a willingness to contribute to others' projects.</li>
    <li>Open, clear, and transparent communication.</li>
    <li>Strong motivation to advance science and engineering through rigorous research.</li>
  </ul>
